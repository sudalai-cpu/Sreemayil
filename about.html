Our Journey

2017 – A Personal Commitment to Natural Living

Our journey began in 2017, when we made a conscious decision to embrace a healthier and more natural lifestyle. We started using herbal and traditional products within our own family, inspired by the belief that nature offers the best solutions for health and well-being.

2018 – From Home Remedies to Community Trust

As our interest in herbal living grew, we learned to prepare a wide range of homemade herbal products using traditional methods and natural ingredients.

What started as a personal practice soon became a community effort. We began sharing these products free of cost with our family members, friends, neighbours, and colleagues. Their encouraging feedback, appreciation, and growing demand motivated us to take the next step.

In June 2018, this humble initiative was officially established as SreeMayil Tharcharbhu Santhai, with a vision to promote self-reliance through natural and traditional products.

2022 – Inspired by Thiru. G. Nammalvar's Vision

The inspiring speeches and philosophy of Thiru. G. Nammalvar strengthened our commitment to natural farming, traditional wisdom, and sustainable living.

With this renewed vision, SreeMayil Tharcharbhu Santhai expanded its mission beyond herbal products to include education, hands-on training, and community awareness. Our goal became clear—to revive traditional knowledge and make it accessible to everyone through practical learning, natural products, and skill development.

2023 – The Birth of SreeMayil Lifestyle Centre

As our activities continued to grow, we took another significant step by purchasing 1.5 acres of agricultural land at Thoranavavi Village. This space was envisioned as a living model of sustainable life—a place where people could learn, experience, and reconnect with nature.

This marked the establishment of SreeMayil Lifestyle Centre, a dedicated campus for natural living, traditional wellness, and entrepreneurship development.

Today, our centre offers training and practical education in:

  • 🌿 Herbal Soap Making
  • 🌿 Herbal Health Care & Personal Care Products
  • 🌿 Organic Farming Practices
  • 🌿 Entrepreneurship Development
  • 🌿 Skill-Based Training Programs for Schools and Colleges
  • 🌿 Sustainable Living Camp
  • 🌿 Varma Therapy Camp
