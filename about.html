Our Beginning

The Story Behind SreeMayil

SreeMayil Lifestyle Centre was established with a singular vision: to revive the rich heritage of traditional knowledge and connect it with modern, daily needs. In a world increasingly dependent on synthetic and chemical-laden products, we set out to create natural, safe, and ecofriendly alternatives.

Our journey began by exploring native botanical formulations and sustainable agricultural practices. We realized that true wellness starts with clean ingredients—which led us to formulate 100% natural herbal soaps, skincare remedies, and household products.

However, SreeMayil is more than just a brand for natural products. We recognized that the path to a healthier planet lies in community empowerment. We transformed our learnings into a comprehensive training center, equipping students, farmers, and aspiring entrepreneurs with hands-on formulation skills, organic cultivation methods, and guidance for starting their own natural businesses.
