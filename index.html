About Us

Dedicated to Natural Living and Rural Empowerment

Located in the serene surroundings of Thoranavavi, Erode, Sreemayil Lifestyle Centre is dedicated to the promotion of natural living and holistic wellness.

Our mission is to empower individuals through traditional herbal knowledge, supporting self-employment, and fostering a community that values chemical-free, sustainable lifestyles.
