Herbal Soap Manufacturing

Empowering the next generation of eco-entrepreneurs with practical knowledge and traditional wisdom.

Course Overview

This is a hands-on practical training course meticulously designed for students and aspiring entrepreneurs. Our program bridges the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical skill, providing participants with the tools they need to launch their own sustainable ventures.

Objectives

  • Practical knowledge in natural soap making
  • Promoting entrepreneurial skills
  • Sustainable product development

Duration & Schedule

10 Hours total, conducted over 2 Days (6 hrs + 4 hrs) for deep practical immersion.

Training Syllabus

A comprehensive 7-module journey from basics to business.

Module 01

Introduction

Fundamentals of natural living and the science of soap.

Module 02

Raw Materials

Understanding oils, butters, and herbal additives.

Module 03

Cold Process

Mastering the traditional cold process manufacturing method.

Module 04

Herbal Value Addition

Infusing traditional medicinal herbs into your products.

Module 05

Packaging & Branding

Eco-friendly packaging and brand identity creation.

Module 06

Entrepreneurship

Business setup, legalities, and rural marketing strategies.

Module 07

Interactive Q&A

Clearing doubts and finalizing your business roadmap with expert guidance.
